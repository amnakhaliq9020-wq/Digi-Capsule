Executive Summary
Amna will deliver three workstreams: (1) a Contacts page, (2) a Home Dashboard, and (3) the wording for three warning emails and three warning SMS messages. Development starts with mock data. Amna must obtain Zamin’s explicit approval before connecting any page to his backend. Accounts, memories, check-in schedules, warning dispatch, and delivery logic are owned by Zamin.
1. Project Overview
Digi Capsule is a digital memory website. People save letters, photos, and videos for their families. Memories are delivered either on a chosen date or through the defined missed-check-in warning process.

4. Task 1: Contacts Page — Start Here
Goal: Let signed-in users manage the people who may receive their memories. Keep the screen clean, simple, and responsive.
4.1 UI Elements
Page heading: “My Contacts”.
Primary button: “Add Contact”.
Contacts list or table with Edit and Delete actions for each entry.
4.2 Form Fields

Rule

Accept international numbers, such as +92…
Delivery channel

4.3 Actions
Add: Once saved, the new contact immediately appears in the list.
Edit: Open a form prefilled with the existing details.
Delete: Require confirmation, then update the list.
4.4 Screen States
Support an empty list, field validation errors, saving/loading state, save failure and success feedback. On mobile, replace the table with stacked contact cards. Begin using mock data; connect user-scoped APIs only after Zamin approves.
4.5 Acceptance Tests

“No contacts” message and Add Contact button appear.
Access isolation
A user cannot view or modify another user’s contacts; verify with Zamin’s backend.

6.3 Suggested Message Templates
Warning 1
Email subject: Digi Capsule: Check-in reminder
Email body: Hi {first_name}, your Digi Capsule check-in is due. Please sign in and press “I’m alive” to keep your saved memories private. Check in here: {secure_link}. If you recently checked in, no action is needed.
SMS: Digi Capsule: Your check-in is due. Press “I’m alive” here: {secure_link}.
Warning 2
Email subject: Digi Capsule: Second check-in reminder
Email body: Hi {first_name}, we still have not received your check-in. Please sign in and press “I’m alive” so your scheduled warning process stops. Open Digi Capsule: {secure_link}.
SMS: Digi Capsule: Second reminder. Please check in to stop pending warnings: {secure_link}.
Final Warning
Email subject: Digi Capsule: Final check-in warning
Email body: Hi {first_name}, this is your final reminder. If no check-in is received within 7 days, memories marked “If I am gone” may be sent to your selected contacts under your settings. Press “I’m alive” now to stop this: {secure_link}.
SMS: Digi Capsule FINAL reminder: Check in within 7 days to stop eligible memory delivery: {secure_link}.
Approval note: Messages must never state that the user has died. Zamin is responsible for reviewing timing, fallback procedures, consent and safety before any messages are sent.
7. Integration and Ownership Rules
Build the frontend first with mock data and agreed API types.
Before connecting a page, share its preview, needed endpoints, fields and test cases with Zamin, and get explicit approval.
Zamin provides authentication context and secure endpoints. Never store secret API keys in the frontend.
Create/edit/delete operations must be restricted to the authenticated user.
Access memories and dashboard data only through authorized APIs.
Warning templates require placeholder, readability and final approval before Zamin schedules them.
8. Final Handoff Checklist
Contacts create/edit/delete works with two recipients, field validation and mobile layouts.
Dashboard shows the last check-in, next due date, memories, contacts, and empty/loading/error states.
All three email and all three SMS templates are finalized and approved.
All integrations have Zamin’s approval; unresolved APIs are documented.
Smoke test: pressing “I’m Alive” sends nothing. Zamin tests delayed “If I am gone” delivery and scheduled video delivery using an accelerated staging clock without sending actual customer messages.
